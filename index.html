AgentMarketSurvey

You are probably here because our bot visited your website. This page explains what it does.

What it collects

For each property an agent advertises for sale or to let, homes and commercial property, the bot records only information shown on the agent's public listing: the address, the asking price or rent, the size or number of bedrooms, the type of property, its status (for example for sale, under offer, sold subject to contract or let agreed), the date it was first seen and which branch handles it.

It also records the agency's office contact details and logo, and, where the agency publishes them on its website, the names, job titles, professional biographies and work contact details of its staff, so that the agency can be contacted about its business. It does not record photographs, home addresses or personal contact details, or any information about buyers, sellers or tenants. It keeps copies of some public pages so that its readings can be checked.

We also use public registers: Companies House (business roles only, never addresses) and regulators' registers.

To have your details removed, email agentmarketsurvey@gmail.com and we will delete them within one working day.

How it behaves

  • It reads each website about once a week, and occasionally a single page on another day to check a reading.
  • It waits at least 5 seconds between requests to the same website.
  • It identifies itself as AgentMarketSurvey/1.0, follows your robots.txt and any crawl-delay, and skips a website whose robots.txt cannot be read.
  • It stops requesting a site at the first refusal (such as a 403 or 429 error) and does not try to get around it, or around any bot check.

What it is for

Summaries of property market activity by estate agents and commercial property agents, for the law firms we work with.

Opt out or ask a question

Email agentmarketsurvey@gmail.com. To stop the bot visiting your site, add User-agent: AgentMarketSurvey and Disallow: / to your robots.txt.
