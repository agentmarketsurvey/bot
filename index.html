AgentMarketSurvey

You are probably here because our bot visited your website. This page explains what it does.

What it collects

For each property advertised for sale, the bot records only information shown on the agent's public listing: the address, asking price, number of bedrooms, whether it is for sale or under offer, the date it was first seen, and which branch of the agent handles it.

Where the listing names the consultant dealing with a property and shows their work email address, or shows a branch email address, the bot records that name and address so that the agent can be contacted about their business. It does not collect personal phone numbers, photographs, descriptions, or any information about buyers or sellers. To have your details removed, email agentmarketsurvey@gmail.com and we will delete them within one working day.

How it behaves

  • It visits once a day, with at least 5 seconds between requests.
  • It identifies itself as AgentMarketSurvey/1.0 and follows your robots.txt and any crawl-delay.
  • It stops requesting a site if the site answers with an error such as 403 or 429.

What it is for

A weekly summary of market activity by estate agents.
